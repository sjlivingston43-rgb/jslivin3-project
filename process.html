How It Works

Watch the Brand Launch Process

This short video introduces the five basic steps involved in launching a supplement brand.

1. Choose Your Products

Start by deciding what types of supplements you want to offer. Consider your target customer and the products that best fit your brand.

2. Build Your Brand

Create a brand name, logo, and visual style that will help customers recognize your business and understand what it represents.

3. Create Your Online Store

Build an online store where customers can learn about your products, place orders, and connect with your brand.

4. Connect Fulfillment

An on-demand fulfillment model allows products to be prepared and shipped when customers place orders, reducing the need to keep a large inventory on hand.

5. Market and Grow

Use digital marketing, social media, and useful content to reach potential customers and continue growing your brand.
