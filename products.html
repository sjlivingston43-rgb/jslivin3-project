Choosing Your Products

Choosing the right products is an important step when building a supplement brand. Different supplement categories can appeal to different types of customers and health and wellness goals.

The table below provides examples of several product categories that an entrepreneur could consider when developing a private-label supplement brand.

Product Category Example Product Target Customer
Sports Nutrition Protein Powder Fitness and active lifestyle customers
Vitamins & Wellness Daily Multivitamin General health and wellness customers
Weight Management Meal Replacement Powder Customers focused on nutrition and weight management
