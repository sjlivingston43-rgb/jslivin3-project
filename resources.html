Helpful Resources

Building a supplement brand requires research, planning, and an understanding of the rules that apply to dietary supplements. The resources below can help entrepreneurs learn more about the industry and operating an online business.

FDA Dietary Supplement Information

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration provides information about dietary supplements, labeling, ingredients, and regulations.

Visit the FDA Dietary Supplements website

Small Business Resources

The U.S. Small Business Administration provides information for entrepreneurs about planning, launching, managing, and growing a business.

Visit the U.S. Small Business Administration website

Continue Learning

Researching your market and understanding your customers can help you make better decisions when selecting products, developing your brand, and creating a marketing strategy.
